Smart enhancement chain

Obvious improvement, controlled gain.

Pre-chain headroom

Hot material is attenuated before EQ, compression, colour, and stereo processing instead of being repaired after distortion occurs.

Context-aware presets

Master, Mix Bus, Track, and Creative presets use different loudness, headroom, stereo, makeup, and limiter policies.

Mas Ari Signature

The default signature keeps the luxurious, lively, 3D, tickling identity with a controlled target around +4 dB when headroom permits.

Independent utility trims

Input and Output remain centred at 0 dB and stay independent from preset recall and A/B creative snapshots.
